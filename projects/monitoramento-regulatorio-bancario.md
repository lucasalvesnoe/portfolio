## What it is

RegulaTech BR is a banking regulation monitoring system for a state development bank, at version 1.1.0. It tracks regulatory changes in the financial sector and links them to what the bank needs to do about them. Organised into `components`, `contexts` and `services`, with versioned documentation alongside.
