## What it is

A screening dashboard that uses AI to read professional training certificates, validate the issuing institution and give the operator a confidence assessment, speeding up the granting of the professional discount without taking the decision out of human hands. The repository is the best-kept of the local ones: it has `app`, `components`, `lib`, documentation, a changelog, a context file for AI agents and a project memory folder, with 12 commits of local history.
