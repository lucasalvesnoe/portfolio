## What it is

An intelligent automation system for a state attorney general's office, aimed at the bottleneck in legal work on outstanding tax debt (dívida ativa). It classifies legal documents automatically, raises critical alerts on deadlines and procedures, and segments debtors to prioritise collection, instead of treating the whole portfolio with the same effort. The design considers LGPD (Brazil's GDPR) compliance at every stage and provides for integration with the legal systems the office already uses. React 18 + TypeScript + Vite with Tailwind and shadcn/ui, versioned with SemVer.
