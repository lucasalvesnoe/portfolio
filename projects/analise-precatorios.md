## What it is

A system for automated analysis of court-ordered government debts (precatórios) and legal cases, built on top of Evadocs. It takes in a case, extracts what matters for the decision to buy the credit and delivers a ready-made assessment, replacing manual document-by-document screening. It follows the client's visual identity, taken from their own brand.
