## What it is

A logistics intelligence platform with generative AI for a logistics operator, at version 3.4.0. It covers delivery monitoring, supplier analysis and proactive alerting, and the repository records an architecture comparison, carrier research and the first wave of integration with a national carrier. React 19 + TypeScript on Vite, organised into `views` and `services`, with a versioned changelog.
