## What it is

A logistics intelligence platform with generative AI for a logistics operator, at version 3.4.0. It has the largest code volume of the projects never versioned on GitHub, at about 14.5k lines. It covers delivery monitoring, supplier analysis and proactive alerting, and the repository records an architecture comparison, carrier research and the first wave of integration with a national carrier. React 19 + TypeScript on Vite, organised into `views` and `services`, with a versioned changelog.
