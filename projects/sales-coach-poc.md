## What it is

A sales coach that runs during face-to-face customer service, built for a chain of physical footwear stores. It captures microphone audio in the browser, transcribes the conversation between salesperson and customer live via the Web Speech API in pt-BR, and sends the text to Claude to generate sales guidance while the conversation is still happening — not a report afterwards. The screen is split: transcript on the left, insights on the right. React + Vite + Tailwind.
