## What it is

Prototype of a virtual assistant for the policyholders of a state pension institute, built as a commercial demonstration piece — entirely mocked, and upfront about it. It covers nine real policyholder journeys: proof of life with an emulated redirect to gov.br (Brazil's government sign-in portal: login, paying-authority authorisation, facial recognition); payslip and income statement via the state citizen portal; personal-details update; sick-leave medical assessment; pre-employment medical assessment through the state electronic case-management system; income tax exemption with OCR reading of the medical report; retirement simulation; survivor's pension; and a management dashboard. It runs offline from a single HTML file with CSS, JS and images embedded. What can genuinely be delivered, and what cannot, is set out in a feasibility matrix versioned alongside it — the positioning is a service layer on top of existing systems, not a replacement for them. Screen fidelity is drawn from the official website and the Medical Assessment Manual V2.
