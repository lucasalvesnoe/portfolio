## What it is

Management system for churches, covering the whole administrative cycle rather than just member records. Member management with photo, family and church history, and status tracking (active, inactive, visitor, transferred, deceased); financial control of tithes and offerings with income and expense categorisation and receipt issuing; an events calendar integrated into the dashboard; custom reports with PDF and CSV export. React 18 + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind on Supabase, with light and dark themes.
